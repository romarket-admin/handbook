# Rank Guide

:::info[Info]
This page details the Rank Structure at RoMarket.
:::

### <font color="#9814D9">Owner</font>
- Fourth SHR Rank.
- Owner of RoMarket
- Not obtainable by community members.

### <font color="#b29146">Group Executive</font>
- Third SHR Rank.
- Staff Members who have been noticed by a Group Executive or the Owner.
- Group Executive Assistants who has made significant contributions to RoMarket.

### <font color="#b29146">Group Executive Assistant</font>
- Second SHR Rank.
- Staff Members who have been noticed by a Group Executive or the Owner.
- Group Administrator who has made significant contributions to RoMarket.

### <font color="#b29146">Group Administrator</font>
- First SHR Rank.
- Staff Members who have been noticed by a Group Executive or the Owner.
- Staff Members who have passed the SHR Application.
- Staff Members who have passed the SHR Interview.
- Staff Members who have passed the Departmental Training and Probationary Periods.

### <font color="#af0000">Store Director</font>
- Third HR Rank.
- Staff Members who have been noticed by a member of the Human Resources Department.

### <font color="#af0000">Store Director</font>
- Second HR Rank.
- Staff Members who have been noticed by a member of the Human Resources Department.

### <font color="#af0000">Store Director</font>
- First HR Rank.
- Staff Members who have been noticed by a member of the Human Resources Department.

### <font color="#e67e22">Store Manager</font>
- Third MR Rank.
- Staff Members who have sucessfully completed two Shift Sessions. 

### <font color="#e67e22">Store Supervisor</font>
- Second MR Rank.
- Staff Members who have sucessfully completed one Shift Session. 

### <font color="#e67e22">Head Staff Member</font>
- First MR Rank.  
- Staff Members who have sucessfully completed three Training Sessions. 

### <font color="#f1c40f">Staff Assistant</font>
- Third LR Rank.  
- Staff Members who have sucessfully completed two Training Sessions. 

### <font color="#f1c40f">Senior Associate</font>
- Second LR Rank.
- Staff Members who have sucessfully completed one Training Session. 

### <font color="#f1c40f">Junior Associate</font>
- First LR Rank.  
- New staff members who have not attended a Training Session.

### <font color="#f08c8c">Valued Member</font>
- Members who have contributed to RoMarket in significant capacities.   
- Usually ex-SHR members.  
- Not obtainable by regular community members.

### <font color="#00ddff">Customer</font>
- Lowest Rank at RoMarket.  
- Able to shop at the store.
