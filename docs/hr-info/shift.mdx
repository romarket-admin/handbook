# Shift Sessions

:::info[Info]
This page details all information about Shift Sessions.
:::


### Claiming Shift Sessions
Sessions are claimed for the next day via the [Claiming Channel](https://discord.com/channels/1456118310860095651/1494394754199457923).  
To claim a session, fill out the following information and submit it at 7pm GMT.  
Claims sent before 7pm GMT will not be accepted.  
Claims must have only one sessions per message to allow others a chance to claim.  
```jsx title="Claiming Format"
Host Username:
Co-Host Username:
Shift Date and Time:
```

### Schedule
Schedules for Shift Sessions are available in the [Staff Portal](https://app.firefli.net/workspace/371372280/sessions) or in the [Claiming Channel](https://discord.com/channels/1456118310860095651/1494394754199457923).  
If you are unable to host your shift, please find a replacement, whether it's your co-host or another HR Staff Member.   
Make sure to notify the scheduler by pinging/replying to their message in the [Claiming Channel](https://discord.com/channels/1456118310860095651/1494394754199457923) so that the session can be counted correctly.

### Starting Shift Sessions
Join the game 5 minutes before your shift starts.  
You must start your shift before xx:10.  
To start a shift, open your staff device, go to the 'Shift' tab and click 'Start Shift'.
You must ensure that you are available for the whole 30 minute duration of the shift.

### Promoting at Shift Sessions
You are allowed to promote a maximum of five staff members per shift.  
You can only promote staff members working at a department or checkout.  
Please ensure that they meet the following requirements:  
- Did not view/follow the Host/Co-Host.  
- Has less than 5 different spelling or grammar mistakes in their WHOLE chatlog.  
- Has not joined the game just for the shift.  
- Has used unique greetings.  
- Must be a Head Staff Member or Store Supervisor.  

To rank a player, head to the [Ranking Channel](https://discord.com/channels/1456118310860095651/1494407562320285737) and use the /setrank command.  
If the command does not work, ask another HR to try before pinging an SHR for support.  

Note: If you have not seen any staff members up to requirement, you do not have to promote anyone.


#### Ending Shift Sessions
Once the 30 minute timer has run out, an announcement will be sent concluding your shift.  
After this you are free to leave, take a photo to send in [Photos](https://discord.com/channels/1456118310860095651/1492638686435872968) or continue playing.
