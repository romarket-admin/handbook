# Promotions

:::info[Info]
This page details how to be promoted from each rank as a High Rank Staff Member.
:::

### Corporate Assistant
Corporate Assistants can only be promoted by a member of the Human Resources Department.
To be considered for a promotion, you should:
- Be active in game.
- Be active in Discord.
- Host Sessions.
- Use proper grammar at all times.
- Help out customers.

### Corporate Manager
Corporate Assistants can only be promoted by a member of the Human Resources Department.
To be considered for a promotion, you should:
- Be active in game.
- Be active in Discord.
- Host Sessions.
- Use proper grammar at all times.
- Help out customers.

### Store Director
Corporate Assistants can only be promoted by a Group Executive or the Owner.
To be promoted to an SHR you must:
- Complete the 7 section form.
- Complete a short interview with the Human Resource Overseer.

After this, your application and responses will be reviewed and you will be given your result along with a feedback report.  
If you are sucessful, you will be promoted to a Group Administrator and be part of either the Human Resources or Public Relations Department.  
You will first recieve a 'Trial' role in your respective department, where you must pass a training and probationary period.   
If you do not pass this, you will be ranked back to Store Director.  
If you do pass, you will become a Member of your respective department.
