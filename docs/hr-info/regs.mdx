# Regulations

:::info[Info]
This page details all the regulations that High Rank Staff Members at RoMarket must follow.
:::

### Uniform

High Rank Staff Members must choose out of the following for in game uniform:   
- RoMarket Staff T-Shirt
- RoMarket Staff Jumper
- RoMarket Staff Shirt
- RoMarket Security Vest (only with Security Gamepass)
- Any item in the Group Store marked [U]

Additonally, all HR Members are required to wear legwear that covers at least half of their legs.


### Working
High Rank Staff Members are only allowed to do the following things whilst on duty:
- Restock Shelves
- Clear Rubbish Bins
- Clean Spills
- Arrest Shoplifters (Security Only)
- Work at Departments
- Supervise the Store
- Help Customers


### Quotas
High Rank Staff Members must complete 60 minutes of in-store time a week.  
You can view your quota status using the [Quota Tracker](https://app.firefli.net/workspace/371372280/quotas) in the Staff Portal.  
Failure to complete your quota repeatedly will result in a Noted Warning and possibly a Termination.   


### Inactivity Leave
If you are unable to complete your quota for a period of time, you may request an inactivity leave.  
These can be request through the [Staff Portal](https://app.firefli.net/workspace/371372280/notices) or via the [Human Resources Server](https://discord.com/channels/1457913136945565758/1470886805669679135).  
We offer upto 1 month of leave. However, this may be extended depending on your reason for leave.  
To request an extended leave, please choose 'Other' in the [Staff Portal](https://app.firefli.net/workspace/371372280/notices) or make a ticket in the [Human Resources Server](https://discord.com/channels/1457913136945565758/1470886805669679135).
