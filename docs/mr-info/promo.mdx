# Promotions

:::info[Info]
This page details how to be promoted from each rank as a Middle Rank Staff Member.
:::

### Head Staff Member
Head Staff Members can be promoted by attending a Shift.
To be considered for a promotion, you should:
- Play the game for a period of time before the Shift.
- Have less than 5 different spelling and grammar mistakes in your whole session chatlog.
- Use unique greetings.
- Not view/follow the Host/Co-Host.

### Store Supervisor
Store Supervisors can be promoted by attending a Shift.
To be considered for a promotion, you should:
- Play the game for a period of time before the Shift.
- Have less than 5 different spelling and grammar mistakes in your whole session chatlog.
- Use unique greetings.
- Not view/follow the Host/Co-Host.

### Store Manager
Store Managers can only be promoted by a member of the Human Resources Department.
To be considered for a promotion, you should:
- Be active in game.
- Be active in Discord.
- Use proper grammar at all times.
- Help out customers.
