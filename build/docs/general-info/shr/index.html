SHR Information

Info

This page details information about the SHR Team

Ranks​

There are 4 SHR Ranks at RoMarket as detailed below:

Owner​

  • Overall supervision of all platforms and community.
  • Makes the executive descision in all matters.

Group Executive​

  • Managing all platforms and community.

Group Executive Assistant​

  • Moderating Server, Game and Community.

Group Administrator​

  • Moderating Server, Game and Community.

Departments​

At RoMarket, SHR Members are assigned to one or both departments.
Each department has multiple responsibilities.

Human Resources​

The Human Resources Department has the following responsibilities:

  • Handle Player Reports
  • Handle Player Punishments
  • Handle Player Promotions
  • Supervise Training and Shifts
  • Host and Promote at Mass Shifts
  • Handle HR Tickets/Queries

Public Relations​

The Public Relations Department has the following responsibilities:

  • Create Promotional Media for the Game, Server, and Community
  • Send Daily Interaction Posts in the Engagement Channel
  • Host Events
  • Host and Promote at Mass Shifts
  • Recognise Memmbers for Promotions
  • Handle PR Tickets/Queries
  • Source and Start Partnerships
  • Advertise the Game/Server

Secondary Roles​

Additionally, some SHR Members may have extra roles as detailed below.

Builder​

Builders are responsible for:

  • Creating the Roblox Game.
  • Pushing updates to the game.
  • Managing and restarting servers.

Designer​

Designers are responsible for:

  • Create graphics for RoMarket.
  • Make sure branding is uniform across platforms.

Developer​

Developers are responsible for:

  • Creating and managing Discord Bots.
  • Creating and managing Roblox Bots.
  • Creating and managing Roblox to Discord integrations.
  • Creating and managing off-site platforms.

Contact Us​

To contact us, you can make a Support Ticket in the Discord Server.
If you need to contact us related to a punishment, please use the Human Resources Server instead.
Please consult this handbook first to see if it can solve your query.
